## ABM Meets Performance Marketing

Modern B2B media buying uses granular firmographic data to warm high-tier target accounts before sales rep outreach. Success is measured by Qualified Pipeline Value, not simple lead volume.

## Tactical Data Layering

*   **Bing + LinkedIn Data:** Bidding on Search terms only when searchers match specific job titles and company names.
*   **Gated Whitepapers:** Distributing technical assets via Native discovery networks like Taboola.
*   **ABM suppressed Remarketing:** Excluding warm accounts from brand awareness ads to save margin.
