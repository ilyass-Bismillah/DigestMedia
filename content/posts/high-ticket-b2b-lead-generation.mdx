## The Authority Mechanism

High-ticket B2B leads do not respond well to 15-second social video hooks. Enterprise buyers require long-form, educational authority content that handle technical objections.

## Native ABM Funnel Workflow

### 1. Journalism Headline
Headline formatted like a genuine news story on premium business publishers (CNBC, MSN).

### 2. Educational Advertorial (Pre-Sell)
Long-form article discussing industry problems, data studies, and solution narratives before directing prospects to a gate assets/demo page.

Native scaling requires high-trust agency lines to bypass aggressive editorial review bottlenecks.
