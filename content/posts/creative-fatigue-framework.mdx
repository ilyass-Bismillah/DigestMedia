## The Decay of ROAS

When frequency rises on standard interest audiences, CPMs inflate and conversion stability decays. Combatting fatigue requires continuous creative iteration.

## The High-Spend Creative Testing Framework

### 1. Hook-Level Iteration
Testing 3-5 distinct 2-second video hooks against a single winning body copy and offer.

### 2. Dynamic Placements
Allowing algorithms to arbitrage creative formats across Reels, Feed, and Stories placements to maintain low blended CPMs.
