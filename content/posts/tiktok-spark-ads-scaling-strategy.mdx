## The Power of Social Proof

Spark Ads allow media buyers to run acquisition campaigns using organic TikTok videos, leveraging existing comments, shares, and creator trust.

## Spark Ads Scaling Workflow

### 1. Identify Organic Winners
Monitor creator content or brand organic posts with high natural engagement.

### 2. Creator Code Sync
Instruct creator to provide the 12-digit ad authorization code.

### 3. Immediate In-Feed Scale
Sync the creator profile to your TikTok Agency Business Center and scale via In-Feed placements for maximum CPA stability.
