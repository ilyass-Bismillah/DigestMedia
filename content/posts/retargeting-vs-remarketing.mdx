## Clarifying the Terminology

While often used interchangeably, understanding the technical boundary between paid retargeting and owned remarketing is essential for conversion velocity.

## Comparative Tactical Application

| Feature | Paid Retargeting | Owned Remarketing |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Channel** | Social Feeds, Display, Search | Email, SMS, CRM Push |
| **Mechanism** | Third-Party/Server Pixel Events | Customer Database (1P Data) |
| **Goal** | Re-engage Anonymous Prospects | Nurture Identified Leads |

The highest-converting brands suppression-sync these mechanisms, pausing paid ads when a customer interacts with a high-intent email sequence.
