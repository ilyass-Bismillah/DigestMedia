## The Fatal Flaw of Multi-Touch Attribution

 relying on in-platform conversion reporting (like Meta Pixel or Google Ads tag) is dangerous when scaling past $10k/day. Fragmented user journeys and cookie blocking lead to severe double-attribution and flawed budget allocation.

## Why Marketing Mix Modeling (MMM) is the Solution

Marketing Mix Modeling uses aggregate historical statistical regression to determine true profitability without tracking individual users.

### MMM Mathematical Pillars:
*   **Baseline Sales:** Revenue generated without marketing.
*   **Media Lift:** Incremental revenue directly attributed to each paid channel.
*   **Adstock Decay:** The prolonged memory effect of advertising over time.

Securing whitelisted agency accounts mitigates spend caps, giving advertisers full auction liquidity to bid aggressively based on MMM findings.
