## Beyond Standard Pixel Retargeting

Blasting the same ad to all website visitors causes rapid audience fatigue. Scale-ready retargeting requires nuanced segmentation based on behavioral intent.

## The High-Converting Retargeting Grid

### 1. Dynamic Product Ads (DPA)
Automatically serves the exact SKU viewed by the prospect, dynamically matching creative to interest.

### 2. Sequential Storytelling
Tells a brand story over time (e.g., Video Testimonials Day 1-3, Founder Story Day 4-7, Scarcity Offer Day 8-14).

### 3. Suppression Lists
Excluding recent buyers from seeing acquisition ads to save margin.
