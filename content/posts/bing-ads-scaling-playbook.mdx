## The Desktop Advantage

Microsoft Advertising provides access to an older, wealthier, desktop-heavy audience with higher enterprise purchasing power than Google Search.

## Bing Scaling Pillars

### 1. 30-40% Lower CPCs
Far less bidding congestion on high-volume commercial keywords.

### 2. Copilot AI Placements
Sponsored brand integration directly inside conversational AI search queries.

### 3. Microsoft Audience Network (MSAN)
High-intent display ads across Outlook.com, MSN, and Edge browser tabs.
