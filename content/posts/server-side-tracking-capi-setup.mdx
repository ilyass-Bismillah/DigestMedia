## The End of Client-Side Tracking

Browser tracking pixels are stripped by ad blockers and iOS privacy controls, leading to up to 30% event match degradation.

## CAPI Structural Benefits

### 1. Server-Side Data Flow
Sending deterministic customer signals directly to Meta's servers, bypassing browser limitations.

### 2. Restoration of Event Match Quality
Restoring event match scores ensures Meta's machine learning algorithm is trained on high-LTV data, reducing CPA and stabilizing ROAS.

### 3. Resilience to Cookie-Blocking
CAPI is 100% immune to current and future browser-based cookie blocking initiatives.
