## The Billing Lock Trap

Google automated billing models flag any mismatch in cardholder names, sudden billing address changes, or aggressive spending spikes on new credit cards.

## Resolving Google Billing Suspensions

### 1. Re-verify Payment Profile
Submit all required government IDs and proof-of-address documents immediately.

### 2. Transition to Agency Invoicing
The most stable long-term solution is migrating multi-million dollar ad portfolios to whitelisted agency invoicing profiles with Net-30 credit terms.

Agency ad infrastructure ensures zero billing downtime during scaling phases.
