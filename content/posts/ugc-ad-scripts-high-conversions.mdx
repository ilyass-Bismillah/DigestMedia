## The Patterns of Impulse Buys

TikTok requires rapid pattern interruption. Direct response UGC must disqualify non-buyers early and drive impulse conversions.

## The UGC Scripting Formula

### 1. The Hook (0-2 Seconds)
Dynamic typography, sound effects, or extreme pattern interrupt (e.g., "Stop scrolling if...").

### 2. The Solution-Agitate-Problem (SAP)
Address the core pain point quickly and present your product dynamically as the only solution.

### 3. Specific Call-to-Action (CTA)
Telling the viewer exactly which SKU to select or benefit to look for on the landing page.
