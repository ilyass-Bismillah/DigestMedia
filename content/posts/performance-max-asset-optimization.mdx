## Controllering the Black Box

Performance Max is highly automated. Media engineers must install strict controls to prevent wasted spend and ensure the model prioritizes incremental revenue.

## PMax Optimization Steps

### 1. First-Party Audience Signals
Feeding the PMax model with Deterministic Customer Signal (Enhanced Conversions / CAPI) immediately accelerates the learning phase.

### 2. Account-Level Negative Keywords
Prevent PMax from bidding on brand terms by applying account-level negative keyword lists.

### 3. Asset Group Consolidation
Consolidating asset groups by product category enables faster algorithm training on high LTV customer cohorts.
