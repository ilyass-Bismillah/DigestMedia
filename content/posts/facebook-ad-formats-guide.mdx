## Diversifying Creatives to Win Auctions

Scaling past $50k/day on Meta requires a diverse creative testing matrix to win auctions across different placements without inflating blended CPMs.

## High-Volume Ad Formats

### 1. UGC Hooks (Reels 9:16)
Raw, native-style content that drives pattern interruption and extremely low customer acquisition costs (CAC).

### 2. Interactive Carousels
Ideal for multi-benefit storytelling and DPA retargeting, maintaining high CTRs.

### 3. Comparison Static Graphics
High-clarity, text-on-image graphics that kontrast product benefits against legacy competitors.
